## Acceptance Criteria Mapping

### AC12: Performance with 3 Power-ups (55 FPS Target)

**Scenario Configuration**:
- Power-ups: 3 active simultaneously
- Balls: 2 active
- Blocks: 50
- Particles: 100
- Projectiles: 10 (if Laser Gun active)
- Target FPS: ≥55
- Duration: 10 seconds

**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Quality auto-adjust enabled
- If FPS drops below 50, automatically switch to MEDIUM quality
- If FPS drops below 45, automatically switch to LOW quality

**Expected Outcome**: PASS with quality settings assistance

### AC13: Performance with 5 Power-ups (50 FPS Target)

**Expected Outcome**: PASS - Memory usage stays well below 250MB

## Quality Settings Impact

### HIGH Quality (Default)
- All visual effects enabled
- Maximum particle density
- Glow effects enabled
- Target: 60 FPS baseline

### MEDIUM Quality (Auto-adjust at <50 FPS)
- Reduced particle density (50%)
- Glow effects disabled
- Visual effects simplified
- Target: 55 FPS

### LOW Quality (Auto-adjust at <45 FPS)
- Minimal particle density (25%)
- No glow effects
- Essential visual feedback only
- Maximum projectile limit reduced
- Target: 50 FPS
